about me


I am a Professor at the School of Mathematics of University of Birmingham, and a member of the Statistics and Data Science Group. I received the B.Sc. degree in Applied Mathematics from Sun Yat-sen University and the PhD degree in Mathematics from SUNY Buffalo. After my PhD degree, I did postdoctoral research at Northwestern University and MIT respectively. I subsequently held faculty positions at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and University of Liverpool, before taking up my current post at Birmingham.

My main research interests are in the interface of Computational Mathematics, Artificial Intelligence and Statistics.
